  • Move WASD / arrows — translate only (left stick on phone)
  • Look Drag the sky — never the stick
  • Up / Down In flight: E · Space / Q · Ctrl (thruster pads on phone). On the ground E is talk, not up.
  • Roll Z / C · Boost Shift / ⚡ · Scroll thrusts along look · pinch zooms the chase view
  • LEARN / LIVE Same Sol, one app — LEARN = planetarium · tours · science · relax; LIVE = PILOT · land · docks · crew RPG · quests.
  • FLOAT / PILOT Keys 1 · 2 — flight feel inside either product. FLOAT = lazy-river idle; PILOT = manual 6DOF.
  • Gears [ ] or , . — DOCK / CRUISE / BURN / TRANSIT speed bands. Hold ⚡/Shift for boost ramp (not binary).
  • Clock Orbits only: 0 pause · 3 real · 4 cruise · 5 fast · 6 cine — you still fly
  • Pause Freezes ship and orbits (Ctrl+Space). Clock ⏸ is orbits-only.
  • Travel Sun, planets, moons, dwarfs (Vesta/Ceres/Haumea/Eris…), Home
  • Skim / Walk Soft hover; soft-land to walk rocks & worlds; thrust up / boost takeoff. Sun skim-only.
